Vegan Style Foods LLC, headquartered in Lake Oswego, OR, operates at the pinnacle of the specialty food sector. Our approach to retail is defined by a curated selection of organic, ethically sourced, and high-performance nutritional products.

We believe that gourmet food should not compromise on values. Every item in our catalog is rigorously vetted for environmental impact, ingredient purity, and culinary sophistication.

"Conscious Consumption is the Highest Form of Style."

Our philosophy is rooted in the belief that the global food system is undergoing a fundamental transformation. At Vegan Style Foods LLC, we are not just observers of this change; we are its architects. Our retail initiatives focus on making gourmet plant-based nutrition accessible and desirable.

We work directly with local farmers and innovative food scientists to ensure that our supply chain is as transparent as it is efficient. By leveraging modern technology through our upcoming mobile platforms, we aim to provide a seamless bridge between local producers and the global consumer.

Data Privacy & Guidelines

Effective Date: May 8, 2026

Vegan Style Foods LLC ("The Company") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This privacy policy describes our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information in connection with our retail mobile applications and website.

Information Collection: We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and shipping information when you use our services. We use this data only to fulfill orders and enhance the user experience of our app.

Third-Party Compliance: We do not sell or trade your data. All transactions are processed through secure, encrypted payment gateways in accordance with global financial standards.
